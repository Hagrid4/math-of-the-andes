SDI Calculator

The SDI calculator will calculate the biodiversity of the Andes mountains based on the population of each animal present. Start by selecting the number of species present in the area.


Number of Species:


    For example, imagine there are 2 species: Chinchilla and Andean Condor.
    

    For Numerator:
    Population of Chinchilla = 13
    Population of Andean Condor = 15
    So n(n-1) for Chinchilla = 13(13-1) = 156
    and n(n-1) for Andean Condor = 15(15-1) = 210
    Hence the numerator will be: 210 + 156 = 366
    

    For Denominator:
    N = total population
    N = 13 + 15 = 28
    N(N-1) = 28(28-1) = 756
    

    SDI = 1 - (366/756)
        = 0.516 (to 3 decimal points)
